# Project overview

## Purpose

Reduce coordination overhead for a senior computer engineering team by bringing meeting reminders, meeting records, and Jira work summaries into Discord.

## Initial scope

| Capability | Behavior | Status | Acceptance criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting setup | Authorized members configure a meeting time, IANA time zone, destination text channel, voice channel, and reminder offsets. | Implemented offline in [PR #15](https://github.com/RileyHop1/JDR_Team_Management_bot/pull/15) under provisional [decision 0002](decisions/0002-meeting-reminders.md). An omitted zone uses the stored guild zone from [PR #17](https://github.com/RileyHop1/JDR_Team_Management_bot/pull/17), then `BOT_TIMEZONE`. The live reminder check and team confirmation are still required. | Invalid schedules and inaccessible channels are rejected with useful messages. An authorized member can view, update, and cancel the meeting. |
| Reminders | Deliver notices at configured offsets before each meeting. | Implemented offline in [PR #15](https://github.com/RileyHop1/JDR_Team_Management_bot/pull/15) on that same provisional policy. A private-server demonstration is still required. | A fake-clock test verifies timing, cancellation, restart recovery, and duplicate handling. Delivery failures are visible to maintainers. |
| Meeting transcripts | Join a supported voice meeting following explicit authorization, capture permitted audio, transcribe it, and publish to the configured meetings channel. | [PR #4](https://github.com/RileyHop1/JDR_Team_Management_bot/pull/4) merged the planning document only. Retention is approved in [decision 0007](decisions/0007-meeting-data-retention.md): meeting content is deleted within 30 days of capture end. Implementation has not started. Issue [#8](https://github.com/RileyHop1/JDR_Team_Management_bot/issues/8) is open. | The bot announces recording, offers a stop control, handles disconnects, and delivers a synthetic-audio transcript. Long transcripts use a private channel attachment with the same access restrictions. |
| Morning brief | At a configured local time, summarize Jira issues in the team's selected in-progress statuses. | Implemented offline in [PR #16](https://github.com/RileyHop1/JDR_Team_Management_bot/pull/16) under provisional [decision 0003](decisions/0003-jira-morning-brief.md). [PR #17](https://github.com/RileyHop1/JDR_Team_Management_bot/pull/17) lets a stored guild channel, time, and zone override the environment. The live Jira and Discord check and team confirmation are still required. | Include issue key, title, assignee or “unassigned,” status, and issue URL; handle pagination, no matching work, and provider failure distinctly. |

The brief initially reflects current Jira state. Historical changes, blockers, or sprint progress require explicit query rules and additional data; the bot must not invent them. Transcript summaries and action-item extraction are optional later features, separate from faithful transcription.

## Out of scope initially

Autonomous Jira edits, attendance grading, covert recording, general-purpose chat, and joining third-party conferencing platforms. Calendar synchronization, recurring meeting rules, and AI-generated summaries require separate decisions.

## Definition of done

A feature meets its acceptance criteria, passes the configured quality checks, has teammate review, documents configuration and operation, and has demonstrated failure handling. Integration features also need a controlled end-to-end check with test resources. Missing credentials or a mocked provider do not count as a successful live integration.
